Empowering the African Farmer

Technology should solve real problems, not just exist in theory. My approach to Agri-Tech strategy is informed by my own experience as a small-scale farmer, ensuring solutions are practical, scalable, and deeply rooted in the realities of African agriculture.

Scaling Tech Solutions for Sustainable Impact

I specialize in taking AI-driven platforms and translating them into tangible tools that improve yields, optimize supply chains, and empower farming cooperatives.

  • Agri-Tech Scaling: Leading growth and onboarding strategies for platforms like "Farmers Assistant," expanding active user bases through localized training.
  • Strategic Partnerships: Identifying market gaps, driving user adoption, and co-developing comprehensive investment pitch decks to secure venture funding.
  • Sustainable Operations: Managing end-to-end supply chains for physical agricultural enterprises, such as industrial hive construction and honey production via Barnview & Co.

By combining high-level data strategy with a hands-on understanding of climate resilience and sustainability, I help build agricultural ventures that are both technologically advanced and economically viable.
