Translating Data into Strategy

In today's fast-paced economic environment, raw data isn't enough. My services focus on transforming complex datasets into actionable, risk-adjusted strategies that guarantee long-term portfolio sustainability.

Bridging Statistical Theory and Real-World Financial Sustainability

My approach to quantitative risk modeling is deeply rooted in my background in Actuarial Science and Data Science. I specialize in auditing and validating massive financial models to ensure organizations can make data-backed investment decisions with confidence.

  • Financial Model Auditing: Validating the core assumptions driving large-scale finance decisions, such as my work with the National Affordable Housing Program.
  • Sensitivity Analysis: Stress-testing payment structures (like Tenant Purchase Schemes) to forecast and mitigate future vulnerabilities.
  • Stochastic Processes: Applying advanced probability models to evaluate long-term financial risks and ensure portfolio stability.

Whether it involves running simulations to assess market volatility or executing mortality grading using advanced frailty models, my goal is to provide a clear, mathematically sound picture of an organization's financial health.

Beyond the numbers, I ensure that all models are reproducible, transparent, and aligned with the overarching strategic goals of the business therefore resulting in measurable increases in efficiency and sustainable growth.
